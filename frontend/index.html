How it works
  1. Staff create their restaurant once in the admin dashboard and print its QR code.
  2. Customers scan it (or pick it from the Join a queue page), enter their name + WhatsApp/email, and get a token.
  3. They watch their live position on a status page, and get auto-alerted when they're close.
  4. Staff tap "Advance to next order" at the counter as they serve each order — that's it.
