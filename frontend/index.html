QueueFlow

Stop making people stand around for an hour.

Customers scan a QR code, get a token, and walk away. QueueFlow tracks live position and pings them on WhatsApp or email the moment their order is close — so line 57 doesn't have to babysit the counter while order 38 is served.

How it works
  1. You create your restaurant once in the admin dashboard and print its QR code.
  2. Customers scan it, enter their name + WhatsApp/email, and get a token.
  3. They watch their live position on a status page, and get auto-alerted when they're close.
  4. You tap "Advance to next order" at the counter as you serve each order — that's it.
